## Use case

The article demonstrates how to leverage casual conversation in a direct message to uncover an email address and its associated Gravatar profile, highlighting the risks of email hashing and the potential for information disclosure through OSINT techniques.

## Steps to test

1. Identify a target email address from a conversation.
2. Hash the email address using both MD5 and SHA256 algorithms.
3. Construct the Gravatar profile URL using the hashed email: https://gravatar.com/<hash>.
4. Access the constructed URL to retrieve the public profile associated with the email hash.
5. Look for any sensitive information or flags within the profile.
