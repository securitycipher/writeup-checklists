## Use case

Favicon hashing can reveal the underlying technology stack of a target, which may expose known vulnerabilities and misconfigurations. This technique is particularly useful for identifying forgotten or less-maintained assets that may be high-value targets.

## Steps to test

1. Perform subdomain enumeration and live host probing to identify potential targets.
2. Fetch the favicon from each identified host using a script or tool.
3. Hash the favicon using MurmurHash3 to generate a unique fingerprint.
4. Compare the generated hash against a known fingerprint dictionary to identify the technology stack.
5. Prioritize targets based on the identified technology and known vulnerabilities.
