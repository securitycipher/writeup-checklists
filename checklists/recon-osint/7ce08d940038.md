## Use case

The article provides a comprehensive methodology for mapping an organization's attack surface, which is crucial for identifying potential vulnerabilities across various exposed assets and services. Understanding the attack surface helps hunters focus their testing efforts on the most critical areas that may contain security flaws.

## Steps to test

1. Define the authorization boundary for the target organization.
2. Use tools like Amass to discover domains and subdomains associated with the target.
3. Classify each discovered hostname based on its DNS resolution and HTTP response.
4. Identify and document application entry points, including URLs, parameters, and authentication requirements.
