## Use case

The article discusses using TrinetLayer for deep reconnaissance starting from a domain to discover subdomains and analyze JavaScript files for potential secrets. This matters because it enhances the efficiency of finding sensitive information that could lead to vulnerabilities.

## Steps to test

1. Visit the TrinetLayer JS Analyzer.
2. Enter the authorized domain you want to analyze.
3. Click 'START NEW SCAN' and wait for the scan to finish.
4. Navigate to the 'Reports' section to view discovered subdomains and findings.
5. Click on any potential secret to view its details, including type and context.
6. Use the Secret Validator to check the validity of discovered secrets.
