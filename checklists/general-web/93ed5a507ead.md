## Use case

HackerOne has facilitated a significant increase in rewards for ethical hackers, highlighting the importance of identifying and reporting vulnerabilities. This trend underscores the need for organizations to prioritize vulnerability management and timely remediation.

## Steps to test

1. Identify a web application that utilizes a bug bounty program.
2. Review the application for common vulnerabilities such as XSS, SQL Injection, or improper access controls.
3. Attempt to exploit identified vulnerabilities to confirm their existence.
4. Document the steps taken to reproduce the vulnerability, including any payloads used.
