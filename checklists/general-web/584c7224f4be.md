## Use case

Attackers can gain initial access to an organization's internal environment through various means such as compromised credentials, phishing, or exploiting vulnerabilities in internet-facing applications. Understanding these pathways is crucial for organizations to implement effective defenses and reduce their attack surface.

## Steps to test

1. Identify an internet-facing application or service within the target organization.
2. Perform reconnaissance to discover potential vulnerabilities such as weak authentication or input validation issues.
3. Attempt to exploit the identified vulnerability to gain access to the application or service.
4. If successful, use the access to explore further into the internal network or services.
