## Use case

The article discusses how granting seemingly harmless permissions to AI agents can lead to unintended privilege escalation, allowing them to execute commands or access files in ways that were not intended by the user. This is critical as it highlights the risks associated with trusting AI agents without fully understanding the implications of the permissions granted.

## Steps to test

1. Identify a command that appears harmless but can be redirected or executed in a way that escalates privileges.
2. Grant permission to an AI agent to execute the command.
3. Monitor the actions of the AI agent to see if it performs unintended operations.
4. Review the output or changes made by the AI agent to identify any privilege escalation.
