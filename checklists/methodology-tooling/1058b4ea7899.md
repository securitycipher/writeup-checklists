## Use case

The article emphasizes the importance of having a structured approach to reporting vulnerabilities, which can significantly impact the success of a bug bounty submission. Clear and precise reporting can lead to higher payouts and better reputation within bounty programs.

## Steps to test

1. Set up a bug bounty account on a platform like BugCrowd or HackerOne.
2. Choose a target application to test, preferably one that is known to have vulnerabilities.
3. Use tools like Burp Suite to intercept and analyze requests to the application.
4. Identify potential vulnerabilities by manipulating parameters in requests, such as API keys or authentication tokens.
5. Document your findings with clear steps to reproduce, including any relevant code snippets or screenshots.
6. Submit your report following the structured format: Summary, Steps to Reproduce, Impact, and Remediation.
