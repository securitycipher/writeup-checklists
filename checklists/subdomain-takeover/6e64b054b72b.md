## Use case

The article discusses a subdomain takeover vulnerability where an attacker can gain control over a subdomain that is not properly configured, allowing them to serve malicious content or intercept traffic. This is critical as it can lead to phishing attacks or data breaches.

## Steps to test

1. Identify a subdomain that is pointing to a service that has been decommissioned or is no longer in use.
2. Check if the DNS records for the subdomain are still active and if they point to a service that can be claimed.
3. Attempt to claim the service associated with the subdomain by registering it on the service provider's platform.
4. Once claimed, configure the service to serve content or redirect traffic to a malicious site.
