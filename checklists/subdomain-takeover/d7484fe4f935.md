## Use case

Featurebase allows users to create custom subdomains without verifying ownership, leading to potential subdomain takeover if the original owner fails to renew their plan or remove their CNAME record. This vulnerability can allow attackers to serve malicious content under a legitimate subdomain.

## Steps to test

1. Register for a Featurebase account and create a paid Growth or higher plan, or use the free Growth trial.
2. Add a CNAME record in your DNS pointing to customdomains.featurebase.app.
3. Let the plan expire or fail to remove the CNAME record.
4. Create a new Featurebase account and point a custom domain at the victim's dangling CNAME record.
5. Confirm that the CNAME exists and that Featurebase serves your content.

## Commands

```text
dig CNAME [featurebase_subdomain].[target].com | grep customdomains.featurebase.app
```
