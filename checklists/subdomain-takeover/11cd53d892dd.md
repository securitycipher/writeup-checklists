## Use case

Subdomain takeover occurs when a subdomain points to a decommissioned service, allowing an attacker to register that service and control the subdomain. This can lead to phishing attacks or unauthorized content being served under a trusted domain.

## Steps to test

1. Identify the target domain and gather all subdomains using subdomain enumeration tools.
2. Check each subdomain for CNAME records pointing to third-party services.
3. Verify if the service associated with the CNAME record is still active.
4. If the service is inactive, attempt to register the service with the same name to claim the subdomain.

## Commands

subfinder -d example.com -all | anew subs.txt
