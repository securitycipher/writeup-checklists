## Use case

The vulnerability was found in the 'Export My Medical History' feature, where user-controlled input was rendered directly into a PDF without proper sanitization, allowing for HTML injection that could lead to XSS attacks.

## Steps to test

1. Log in to the healthcare platform.
2. Navigate to the profile settings and update your name to include HTML tags, e.g., <h1>Test</h1>.
3. Go to the 'Export My Medical History' feature.
4. Request the PDF export of your account history.
5. Download the generated PDF and open it to observe the rendered HTML.

## Commands

```text
GET https://example.com/export?user_id=YOUR_USER_ID
```
