## Use case

The vulnerability exists in the WasmPlugin resource of Istio, allowing an attacker with create or update permissions to exploit the lack of IP filtering in the HTTP client, potentially accessing sensitive internal services.

## Steps to test

1. Review the Istio codebase, specifically the WasmPlugin controller in pkg/wasm/httpfetcher.go.
2. Identify the instantiation of http.Client without IP filtering.
3. Create a malicious WasmPlugin resource with a URL pointing to an internal service.
4. Apply the WasmPlugin resource to the Istio environment.
5. Check the logs of istiod to confirm that the internal service was accessed.
