## Use case

The application allows one Owner to remove another Owner from the company through an API call, leading to unauthorized access removal for the affected Owner while the removing Owner retains full control. This flaw can be exploited by a malicious Owner to eliminate competition and control company resources.

## Steps to test

1. Create two accounts with Owner roles (Account A and Account B).
2. Authenticate as Account B.
3. Send a DELETE request to /v1/company/<companyUid>/member/<A-memberUid>?companyUid=<companyUid> targeting Account A's member ID.
4. Verify the response is HTTP/2 204 No Content.
5. Log back into Account A and check for access to company resources.

## Commands
