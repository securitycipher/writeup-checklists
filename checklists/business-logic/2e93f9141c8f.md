## Use case

The vulnerability lies in the governance of upgradeable proxy contracts for a Layer 2 blockchain bridge, where a zero-second timelock allows immediate execution of upgrades, risking over $64 million in user funds without any warning or exit opportunity.

## Steps to test

1. Identify the upgrade governance structure of the Layer 2 blockchain by querying the relevant contracts.
2. Check who controls the upgrade process and the conditions under which upgrades can occur.
3. Use the command 'cast call <TimelockController_address> "getMinDelay()(uint256)" --rpc-url <rpc_url>' to determine the minDelay value.
4. Verify the upgrade history to confirm if upgrades have been executed with zero delay.
