## Use case

The vulnerability lies in the ability to bypass the free tier message limit by using a shared chat link in conjunction with a VPN to change the IP address, allowing users to send unlimited messages without signing up or paying.

## Steps to test

1. Open the AI chatbot in guest mode and start chatting until you hit the free tier message limit.
2. Click the 'Share Chat' button and copy the generated share URL.
3. Activate a VPN to obtain a new IP address.
4. Open the copied shared chat link in a new tab while the VPN is active.
5. Verify that you can send new messages as if it were a fresh session.
6. Repeat the process by using up the new messages, sharing the chat again, and changing the IP.

## Commands

```text
1. Open the AI chatbot in guest mode: https://example.com/chat
2. Share the chat: Click 'Share Chat' and copy the URL.
3. Activate VPN: Use any VPN service to change your IP.
4. Open shared link: https://example.com/shared-chat-link
```
