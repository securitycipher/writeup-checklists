## Use case

The customer support chatbot allows users to bypass the refund limit enforced on the standard return flow, enabling users to exploit the system by obtaining unlimited refunds without human intervention.

## Steps to test

1. Log in to your account on the target platform.
2. Attempt to request a refund for two digital titles through the standard return flow to reach the refund limit.
3. Access the customer support chatbot at [REDACTED]/contactus.
4. Initiate a refund request for a title that exceeds your refund limit through the chatbot.
5. Confirm that the refund is processed successfully despite exceeding the limit.

## Commands

```text
POST [REDACTED]/contactus/chatbot/refund
{ 'title_id': 'example_title_id' }
```
