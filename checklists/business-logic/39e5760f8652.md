## Use case

The API allows for actions that can be automated at a scale not intended by the business, leading to potential abuse of sensitive business flows such as account creation and ticket purchases.

## Steps to test

1. Identify endpoints related to sensitive business flows such as purchases or account creation.
2. Attempt to automate requests to these endpoints at a high frequency using a script.
3. Check for server-side controls like rate limiting or CAPTCHA verification.
4. Observe if the API allows actions that should be limited to human users.
