## Use case

The vulnerability allowed an attacker to associate a victim's email address with an attacker-controlled team by exploiting invitation token leakage and the lack of email ownership verification during account registration. This could lead to unauthorized team membership and actions being performed by the victim without their consent.

## Steps to test

1. Create or control a team within the application.
2. Use the team invitation functionality to invite a victim email address.
3. Capture the invitation token from the API response after sending the invitation.
4. Replace the token in an existing invitation link with the captured token.
5. Use the manipulated invitation link to register an account with the victim's email address.
6. Confirm that the registration does not require email ownership verification.
7. Have the legitimate owner perform a password reset to access the account.
8. Verify that the legitimate owner is now associated with the attacker-controlled team.

## Commands

```text
POST /api/invite -d '{"email": "victim@example.com"}'
GET /api/invitation/response -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
POST /api/register -d '{"email": "victim@example.com", "password": "<password>"}'
```
