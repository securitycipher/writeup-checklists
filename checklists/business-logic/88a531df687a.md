## Use case

The article discusses various business logic vulnerabilities that allow attackers to manipulate pricing and quantities in an e-commerce setting, which can lead to significant financial loss for the business.

## Steps to test

1. Run Burp Suite and enable Intercept.
2. Visit the product page of the item you want to purchase.
3. Click 'Add to Cart' and intercept the request in Burp.
4. Modify the 'price' parameter from 133700 to 1 and click 'Forward'.
5. Go to the cart to verify the price has changed to 0.01.
6. Click 'Place Order' to complete the purchase.

## Commands

```text
GET /add-to-cart?product_id=1&price=133700 HTTP/1.1
Modify to: GET /add-to-cart?product_id=1&price=1 HTTP/1.1
```
