## Use case

The payment logic flaw allowed an attacker to manipulate the payment amount in a QR code, resulting in successful transactions for amounts less than the actual order value. This vulnerability could lead to unauthorized purchases and financial loss for the service provider.

## Steps to test

1. Add an item to the cart on the target application.
2. Proceed to checkout and reach the payment page.
3. Extract the QR code payload and identify the parameter 'am' representing the payment amount.
4. Modify the 'am' parameter from 'am=500' to 'am=1'.
5. Use the modified QR code with a UPI application to initiate the payment.
6. Observe the transaction status and confirm if the payment is accepted despite the amount mismatch.
